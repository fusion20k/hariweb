Our Philosophy

Immersion is how you actually
learn a language

Hari is built on decades of language research: you learn fastest — and remember longest — when you encounter a new language naturally, inside context you already understand. It's why living abroad works, and why flashcards don't. Hari brings that same mechanism to the tabs you already have open.

Comprehensible Input

You see new Tagalog words inside English sentences you already understand. Your brain picks up meaning naturally, without memorization.

Effortless Repetition

As you browse normally, Hari gradually increases Tagalog exposure. Spaced repetition happens automatically — no flashcard grind.

Zero Disruption

No study schedule, no app to open. Hari layers Tagalog into your existing browsing — the same immersion mechanism that makes living abroad the fastest way to learn.

How it works

Four steps. That's it.

No sign-ups to configure, no lessons to schedule. Install Hari and let Tagalog quietly find its way into the tabs you already have open.

  1. 01

    Install the extension

    Add Hari to Chrome in one click. It's free and takes about ten seconds.

  2. 02

    Browse normally

    Keep reading the sites you already love. Tagalog words start appearing naturally inside them.

  3. 03

    Hover for the meaning

    Not sure of a word? Hover to see the translation, pronunciation, and an example sentence.

  4. 04

    Tune your comfort level

    Dial Tagalog up or down anytime. Hari grows with you — from a gentle sprinkle to full immersion.
