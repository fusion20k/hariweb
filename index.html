Built for the Fil-Am who always meant to learn

You didn't grow up with Tagalog. Maybe your parents spoke it but switched to English with you. Maybe you understand a little but can never find the words. Maybe you feel awkward at family gatherings when everyone switches over and you just smile and nod.

Hari isn't another class or flashcard app you'll open twice and abandon. It lives inside your browser and does one simple thing: it layers Tagalog words onto the pages you're already reading. Over time, your brain starts picking them up — because that's how languages are actually absorbed, through repetition in context, not drills.

You control how much Tagalog you see

Use the simple 0–100% slider to decide how much Tagalog gets mixed into each page. Starting at 10% means you'll see just a handful of familiar words per article — easy, low-pressure, and enough to start building recognition.

Inch it up when you're ready. There's no pressure to go fast. The goal isn't to cram — it's to make Tagalog feel a little less foreign every single day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already know some Tagalog?

No. Hari is built for people starting from zero. You set the slider low, and it introduces just a few Tagalog words at a time so your brain can absorb them in context. You'll start recognizing words naturally — no memorization required.

I've tried apps before and burned out. Is this different?

Yes — because Hari doesn't ask you to open anything new or set aside study time. It lives in your browser and works quietly while you do what you already do online. There's nothing to maintain, no streaks to keep, no guilt if you miss a day.

Which sites does Hari work on?

Hari works on most text-based websites — news sites, Twitter/X, Facebook, blogs, and more. Some platforms with heavy restrictions (like certain streaming services) may be limited.

Why isn't Hari completely free?

Each page you browse with Hari on uses paid translation APIs. The $20/month subscription covers those costs so we can keep Hari focused on quality.

Can I cancel anytime?
