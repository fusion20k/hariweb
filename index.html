Our Philosophy

Fluency is built by exposure,
not effort.

Decades of linguistic research point to a single conclusion: the brain acquires language fastest when it meets new words inside familiar context. Hari delivers that exact mechanism — quietly, continuously, inside the websites you already read.

Comprehensible Input

New Tagalog words appear inside English sentences you already understand. Meaning emerges through context — the way real fluency is built.

Effortless Repetition

Hari calibrates exposure as you browse, surfacing the right words at the right intervals. Spaced repetition — without a single flashcard.

Zero Disruption

No lessons, no streaks, no schedule. Hari layers the language into the tabs you already have open — the same immersion that makes living abroad work.
