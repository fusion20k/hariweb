Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already know some Tagalog?

No. Hari is built for absolute beginners. Set the slider low and it introduces a few Tagalog words at a time so your brain absorbs them in context.

I've tried apps before and burned out. Is this different?

Yes — Hari lives in your browser and works while you do what you already do. No streaks, no guilt, nothing to maintain.

Which sites does Hari work on?

Most text-based websites — news sites, Twitter/X, Facebook, blogs, and more. Some heavily restricted platforms may be limited.

Why isn't Hari completely free?

Each page uses paid translation APIs. The $20/month covers those costs so Hari stays high-quality.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from your dashboard in two clicks — no questions asked, no charges after cancellation.

Why do I need a card for the trial?

Every translation has a real cost. A card prevents abuse and lets your subscription continue seamlessly if Hari works for you. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

What happens when I hit 10,000 characters?

Translations pause and you'll get a heads-up. Stay subscribed and they resume at $20/month. Cancel and you won't be charged again.
